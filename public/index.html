Sync across devices

Habits, journal, reading progress and review cards are encrypted here with your passphrase, then saved to a private gist on your GitHub account. Nobody can read the gist without the passphrase, including GitHub.

  1. Create a fine-grained GitHub token. Under Account permissions, set Gists to Read and write. Nothing else is needed.
  2. Paste it below with a passphrase. Use the same passphrase on every device. If you forget it, the synced copy can't be recovered.

Sync is on for this device. Changes upload a couple of seconds after you make them, and other devices' changes come in when you return to the tab.

Headlines link to their publishers. Data refreshes every 15 minutes.
